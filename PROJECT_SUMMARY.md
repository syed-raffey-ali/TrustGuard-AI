# TrustGuard AI — Project Summary

TrustGuard AI is a privacy-first scam interception layer for banks and fintech apps. It protects people at the most neglected stage of fraud: the conversation before money moves. A scammer may combine a fake bank call, an urgent chat, and an OTP request; TrustGuard connects those signals across channels instead of judging each message in isolation.

The working prototype includes a Kotlin/Jetpack Compose Android client, a FastAPI realtime gateway, and a React mission-control dashboard. It combines fast deterministic Tier-1 rules, a bundled local taxonomy classifier, optional Ollama/cloud models, caller context, speech-to-text support, and explainable scores with quoted evidence. Users see why a conversation is suspicious and receive safe actions such as verification, hanging up, reporting, blocking, or demo account freezing.

The system supports English, Roman Urdu, and Urdu-oriented patterns including bank impersonation, urgency, secrecy, credential harvesting, grooming, and prompt injection. The dashboard exposes live sessions, risk timelines, evidence history, model latency, provider health, Paste Analyzer reports, and privacy controls. Analysis remains visible in the Android client; provider keys stay server-side and raw audio is not persisted in the prototype.

TrustGuard AI is not a black-box alarm or a post-transaction report. It is an auditable, user-triggered early-warning system that helps a person pause before an OTP, PIN, card number, or transfer leaves their control. The repository includes a runnable setup script, demo scenarios, tests, an installable APK, a local model artifact, and completed web and Android demonstrations.
